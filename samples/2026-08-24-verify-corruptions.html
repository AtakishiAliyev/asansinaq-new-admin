What the verification wave is asked to spot

Each pair is one fixture question rendered as extracted, beside the same question with a single deliberate corruption — the same damage scripts/verify-smoke.ts injects when it scores the wave. In the real wave the left-hand side is a scanned crop rather than our own render, which makes the comparison harder, not easier.

Judge the pairs yourself before reading the wave's scores. A difference you cannot find here is one the model is unlikely to find either, and that would be a fact about the renderer rather than about the model. The figure-mark row is the one to look at hardest: it is where the wave currently misses most, and where the corrupted figure still looks entirely reasonable on its own.
